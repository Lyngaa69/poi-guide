    Voice tips for iPhone

    Safari only hands web pages some of the voices installed on your iPhone. Voices you download under Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices, especially Enhanced and Premium versions, may not show up here.

    Turn on “Show voices in all languages” to see every voice Safari offers. The line under the voice list shows exactly which voice is in use.
