Before you start
  • Turn off silent mode. iPhone: the switch on the side must not show orange. Android: do not use silent or vibrate mode.
  • Turn the volume up. The guide plays at media volume. Connect the phone to the car by Bluetooth or CarPlay if you want it through the car speakers.
  • Allow location when the phone asks. Without your position the guide cannot tell when you reach a stop.
  • Tap Start guide before you drive and keep this page open. The screen stays on while the guide runs, so the phone does not lock.
  • Dark screen puts a black screen over the app to save battery and avoid glare. The guide keeps running underneath. Tap anywhere to see the screen again.
