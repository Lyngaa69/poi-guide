Voice for the audio

One setting for all routes. When you save, GitHub remakes the audio for all routes with the new voice.

Keep it short, for example “relaxed podcast host, warm and conversational”. Do not write age, gender or accent here. You can copy the style text from Google AI Studio.

Free Gemini has a daily limit (about 10 stops per day for the best model, shown in your own AI Studio). A new voice remakes every stop, so it can take several days. The rest is made automatically every morning.

Try voices and styles in Google AI Studio (a new tab). Expressions such as <breath> are added per stop in step 3.

1. Place the stops

A stop is read out when you drive within the trigger radius of it. Snapping puts the stop on the road so it is always triggered. For a place away from the road, snap a stop onto the nearest point of the route and say in the text where the place is.

2. Publish to Wayside

Filled in automatically: the length, then start and stop. Change it freely and add the places people pass, for example “via Himmelbjerget”. If you have written your own text, it is left alone.

The default radius is used by every stop that is set to “Route default” in step 3.

Use it when you have changed the route in Kurviger. The line is replaced. Your stops, texts, settings, invitations and links stay as they are, and no new audio is needed. Stops that are no longer near the line can be moved on the map. Nothing changes for anyone else until you press Publish.

GitHub connection: not set up

Studio saves the route straight into your repository, so you do not have to upload files by hand. This needs a token. It is stored only in this browser and is never sent anywhere except to GitHub.

How to create the token (once)

  1. On github.com, click your profile picture, then Settings, then Developer settings at the bottom of the menu.
  2. Choose Personal access tokens > Fine-grained tokens > Generate new token.
  3. Name it Wayside Studio and pick an expiry date, for example 90 days.
  4. Under Repository access choose Only select repositories and pick your Wayside repository.
  5. Under Permissions open Repository permissions and set Contents to Read and write.
  6. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it above. Do not share it with anyone, not even in a chat.

Menu names on GitHub may differ a little. If your phone is lost, delete the token under the same settings.

3. Write the texts

Leave a text empty if you want Claude to write it later. Wayside then only reads out the name.

With the Gemini voice you can add expressions such as <breath> or <chuckle> where the cursor is: pick one under a text and press Insert. The phone voice and the Listen button skip them.

Texts for Clipchamp, and audio you made yourself

For when you make the audio in Microsoft Clipchamp (or another program) instead of with Gemini. 1) Make the texts here and paste each part into Clipchamp (Record & create > Text to speech, Danish, your voice). Click at the end of every line of dots and press Enter, so Clipchamp makes it a pause of 2.5 s. Then save. 2) Export the audio only. 3) Choose the file below. GitHub cuts it at the long pauses, makes one file per stop and adds them to the route. Publish the route first, so the texts on GitHub are the ones you pasted.

4. Turn directions

With directions switched on, Wayside shows an arrow and says “In 300 metres, turn right” along the route. The turns below are worked out from the shape of the track, because Wayside has no road map. Check them, and correct what is wrong. Wayside follows the route as planned and does not find another way.
