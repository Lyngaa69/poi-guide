Pronunciation

Listen to the versions of each word and pick the one that sounds best. Your choices are saved in the repository, and GitHub remakes the audio for the stops that use those words. Pick A if the voice already says the word correctly.

The audio currently uses the Gemini voice. Choices made here with lydskrift (IPA between / /) only change the Piper voice. Plain respellings (for example torvet = tårvet) also work with Gemini. No new word list is made while Gemini is on.
